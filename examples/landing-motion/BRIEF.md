# CircularQuay landing design brief

## Identity and purpose

Preserve the CircularQuay logo, existing typography, warm ivory/forest palette and consistent light/dark theme. The landing is a focused one-pane introduction to identity, encryption and application delivery, headed **“Get the right secrets ready.”**

## Composition

Use two authentication nodes, two encrypted sources, four environment targets and a central CircularQuay node labeled Managed policies. Six secondary connections represent age, KMS, signing, SCIM, clients and audit. Product names are bold inside each tile, with a concise purpose beneath a prominent mark. The compact landing links directly to the first featured Explore document; Explore home remains the grouped overview.

The diagram and story remain continuously animated; the horizontal divider between them is removed. All overlays and routes use 2px strokes; the hub base has no border. Text padding is 18px. On phones, node faces use an icon/name/purpose grid with an 18px minimum row gutter. The motto stays on one line at every viewport. Headers use a common stable root gutter and padding; language precedes theme at widths up to 760px so the logo and wordmark remain legible.

The loader fills the viewport, centres the logo, uses a neutral-grey breath and a clockwise arc clipped inside the original C-shaped logo band, then fades logo out and main content in. It is sized at 50vw with a height cap; reduced motion is still. Escape closes a search dropdown without pausing that flow, while the diagram retains its own Escape pause behavior. The footer is one discreet horizontal line at the viewport bottom; very short or zoomed layouts retain natural scrolling for access.

## Documentation and login

Explore home groups 55 documents into six task-oriented paths, with implemented Help distribution and autocomplete. The same compact menu appears on desktop, tablet and foldable landing layouts. The local source freeze contains 59 source files and is based on canonical commit `15f368a9f8ac4b45f5d92772c492e863b0a0f5f9`, with manifest SHA-256 `abb8878ee734dbe398f98c15c8abd22de1e3a511e1620d2de9917f4fd0d16b45`. Current guides use `cq` and `cq-sdk`; historical engineering records are identified and retain their revision-specific evidence.

Login uses the same shell and a searchable thirty-provider wall. Authentication/provisioning, vendor adapters and production legal content remain pending. Detailed validation and design assessment are recorded separately in `CANDIDATE-REVIEW.md`.
