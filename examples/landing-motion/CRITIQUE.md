## Specificity and overall impression

The page is technically tidy but **not accepted against the full integration-breadth brief**. Its generic symbols and mobile aggregation make CircularQuay look narrower than the source study. The score measures usability mechanics, not compliance with that mandatory content requirement. A clean detector result cannot resolve the design failure.

The original study's recovered inventory is **113 entries: 30 identity/authentication, 35 encrypted-source/storage and 48 application examples**. Selected named examples and one guided route may manage attention, but must not replace, hide, collapse or remove the inventory.

## What works

- The local-policy boundary is understandable and does not require a live secret or provider connection.
- The selected logo13-derived assets, exact name/slogan, existing fonts, colours and themes remain intact.
- Pause, replay, direct chapters, reduced motion and ordinary page navigation preserve visitor control.

## Cognitive load and emotional journey

The baseline is low in cognitive load, but it asks visitors to supply their own connection from generic categories to familiar tools. There are no decision points with more than four visible controls. The emotional journey moves from a confident headline to a clear policy route and then a functional workbench link, without making the ecosystem tangible. The correction should direct attention within a rich field, rather than remove the field.

## Priority issues and justified corrections

1. **P1 — Restore recognizable breadth.** Generic file/cloud/repository and service/CI/agent symbols cannot demonstrate the required ecosystem. Preserve all 113 original entries in categorized icon fields at every width, using real marks and names. Qualify the scene as illustrative so visibility is not mistaken for verified adapter support. Applicable guidance: Impeccable animate, layout and clarify.
2. **P1 — Preserve mobile meaning.** The aggregate source and app erase the breadth despite a neat mobile layout. Recompose the full inventory vertically with usable targets, readable selected names and ordinary scrolling. Do not use a carousel, hidden “more” state or generic aggregation as a substitute. Applicable guidance: Impeccable adapt.
3. **P2 — Give exploration a purpose.** Visitors need to recognize their own systems and understand each role. Keep a single readable journey through the full field, with discoverable naming and keyboard/touch access. Flocking is optional; it is not the acceptance criterion. Applicable guidance: Impeccable animate and delight.
4. **P2 — Land the breadth at the close.** Connect the selected systems and local-policy explanation to the existing workbench action. Preserve the synthetic/local privacy boundary and avoid unsupported provider claims. Applicable guidance: Impeccable clarify, then polish.
